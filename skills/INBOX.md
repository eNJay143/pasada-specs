# Contributions inbox

A rough entry beats none. Copy the template, keep your name and date, and say what you observed. Mark an inference or an unverified lead explicitly. Give file paths, revisions or reproducible observations so the next reviewer can check the claim. Include environment variable names only when relevant. Keep secret values out.

| Contribution | Home |
|---|---|
| Best practice or methodology | 03 for code, 10 for delivery, 11 for testing, 14 for workflows |
| Design choice | 01 decision record |
| Shared change rule | 04 |
| Failure story | 06, cross-referenced from 04, 05 and 16 where relevant |
| Debugging tip | 05 |
| Limitation or dependency | 15 or 13 |
| Proven principle | Propose for 16, with the user's decision before promotion |

At refresh, verify each entry or mark it unverified. Move the substance and credit into the owning document before removing the processed entry. Preserve competing accounts with evidence for a user decision. Direct edits are welcome. See [00](00_INDEX.md) for retirement and changelog rules.
